      How to add yours

      1. 1

        Say where

        Let it find you, search the street, or just tap the map. Then drag the pin onto the exact pole.

      2. 2

        Say what

        When, what kind of bike, which lock, where it was parked. Five taps.

      3. 3

        That's it

        Your pin joins the heat map and the picture gets a little clearer for everyone else.
